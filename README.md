# Balancey

**Balancey** is a modern application for managing personal and shared expenses, designed to make splitting bills simple and transparent.

Whether you're sharing groceries with your partner, paying for dinner with friends, or managing expenses during a trip, Balancey helps you keep track of **who paid, who owes, and how much**.

> 🚧 **Balancey is currently in early beta and actively under development.**

## 💰 What is Balancey?

Balancey focuses on a common everyday problem: **splitting expenses fairly without doing manual calculations.**

Instead of simply dividing a bill by the number of people, Balancey allows expenses to be broken down into individual products and assigned to specific participants.

For example:

```text
Pizza        35 zł   → Igor + Anna
Drinks       12 zł   → Everyone
Snacks       18 zł   → Michał
```

The application uses this information to calculate the appropriate share for each person.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👥 Shared Expenses

Create expenses and add the people involved in them.

### 🧾 Product-Based Splitting

Break a bill down into individual products and decide who should pay for each one.

### 💳 Automatic Balances

Balancey calculates who paid too much, who owes money and how much needs to be settled.

### 📊 Expense Overview

Keep track of shared spending and see the current financial balance between participants.

### 📸 Receipt Scanning

Receipt scanning is part of the planned development. The goal is to automatically recognize products and prices from a photo and turn them into an editable expense.

## 🎯 The Goal

Balancey is designed to make shared finances **simple, fast and understandable**.

No spreadsheets.
No calculators.
No trying to remember who paid for what.

Just add the expense, assign the products and let Balancey handle the calculations.
